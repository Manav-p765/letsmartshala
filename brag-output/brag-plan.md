# Brag Plan: SmartShala

## What is this app?
SmartShala is a school management CRM for Indian schools that brings admissions, attendance, fees, exams, transport, payroll, and role-specific views into one system.

## Planning rubric
1. What is the app? A school management CRM for Indian schools, on the web and in principal and teacher apps.
2. Funniest or most impressive claim: "The whole school day, in one system."
3. Visual hook: the paper-to-UI contrast: repeated register work collapses into the day arc and a single student file.
4. Actual UI to show: the home hero day arc, four desks role cards, and the "From admission to fee receipt" student file.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 22.93s, long enough for the register pain, product reveal, role cards, journey, and CTA.
6. Tone: polished; a confident school-office product film with a little dry setup.
7. Audio: steady polished bed with sparse UI drops, a soft reveal hit, and a final logo bell.
8. Share caption: SmartShala turns admissions, attendance, fees, exams, transport and payroll into one school day, in one system.
9. User flow worth showing: add/import student -> place in class -> assign fees -> record payment -> receipt and ledger update.

## The angle
The video treats a familiar school-office problem seriously: one student being rewritten across registers and spreadsheets. SmartShala arrives as the cleanup: one day arc, four role views, and one student file moving from admission to fee receipt.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A paper desk fills the frame. The same student name appears across five registers, then a red mismatch flashes: "one student. five registers."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The paper stack folds into the SmartShala day arc and the line "The whole school day, in one system."
- Four role cards arrive one by one: Principal, Office admin, Teacher, Accountant.
- A sample student file progresses from Admitted to Receipt, ending with a Paid stamp.

## Outro / punchline
SmartShala mark and name land with the line "Book a demo" and the site URL.

## User flow worth showing
Add or import the student -> place them in a class -> assign the fee structure -> record the payment -> receipt and ledger, done.
