## Design decisions (current)

- **Palette ("field notes", branch `palette-ivory`):** light only.
  - Page and surfaces: paper (light ivory) `#F5EFE6`, ivory `#EBE0D2` (the
    `mist` theme and alternate sections), and warm white `#FFFDF8` for cards.
  - Ink and headings: deep navy `#0E1D61`.
  - Gold `#B8964F` is for linework only. Gold you read uses `--gold-ink`
    `#84662A`: eyebrows, the serif accent word and `--hi`.
  - Logo blue `#003DE5` stays for the mark, the "Shala" wordmark, the hero
    and CTA arcs (the owner wants the arc blue), and the button hover. CRM
    blue `#2456E6` is for buttons and links.
  - No dark or bright-blue section backgrounds (the owner rejected them). The
    `navy`, `night` and `blue` themes still exist in `variables.css` but are
    unused.
- **Translucent colours:** always use the channel tokens, as in
  `rgb(var(--navy-rgb) / 0.1)` (also `--blue-rgb`, `--gold-rgb`,
  `--white-rgb`). Never write raw `rgba()` with hard-coded numbers.
- **School sketches:** `.linework` (in `global.css`) is a faint gold
  background of school-object line drawings (`public/school-lines.svg`,
  generated by `scripts/school-lines.mjs`). It appears only in the hero and
  the CTA. The owner wants it subtle and minimal: a few small items in the
  far corners only. It replaced the topographic contour lines, which the
  owner rejected.
- **Hero arc:** blue, standing on straight legs that run to the hero's bottom
  edge on desktop (`dFull` and `dStartLeg` in `arcGeometry.js`). The sun and
  the progress stroke still travel the curve only.
- **Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans everywhere, plus Instrument Serif italic for one
  accent word, JetBrains Mono for labels, and Mukta for Hindi.
- **Section titles** use `--fs-title`, sized by width and capped by height.
- **Logo:** free-standing blue SS letters, traced to a vector
  (`src/components/Brand/markPath.js`), with no box around them. The favicon
  is the boxed version (`public/favicon.svg`).
- **Hero (the signature moment):** a light page with a blurred blue arc. A sun
  crosses it from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM **once**, and does not loop. Small,
  muted cards float gently (about 6px) and hang off the arc on dotted
  strings. The arc must never overlap any text. The role switch (principal,
  teacher, accountant) replays the day only when clicked. No lede paragraph
  and no "sample data" line.
- **Reveals:** `useReveal()` per section. Nothing is hidden by CSS.
  `data-animate="in"` adds `.is-in` through an IntersectionObserver.
- **Pricing rule (owner, 2026-10-12):** never show prices or plan names anywhere
  on the site. To learn pricing, a visitor fills in the form and books a
  meeting. The Pricing page explains what pricing depends on and has the form.
- **Speed pass (2026-10-12):** the live site was sending an empty page (the
  prerender step could not start Chromium on Vercel), so nothing showed until
  all the JavaScript had run. Now: the build prerenders on Vercel with
  `@sparticuz/chromium` and writes `dist/prerender.txt` ("ok: N pages" or
  why it was skipped — check it at `/prerender.txt` after a deploy); the app
  takes over saved HTML without a blink ("static boot" in `lib/motion.js`,
  `main.jsx` preloads the page's code first); home sections below the first
  screen use `content-visibility: auto`; two fonts are preloaded and the
  Mukta font was dropped (the footer's Hindi word uses the device font);
  other pages' code is prefetched only after load, never on slow connections.
  Measure with `node scripts/perf.mjs <url> home demo about pricing`.
- **Demo page (owner, 2026-10-12):** `/demo` is the home page with a
  different hero: its own hero with the form, then every home section in the
  same order. The "What happens next" block was removed to follow that (its
  text is still in `data/pages.js` → `demo.next`, `demo.covers`).
- **Social (2026-10-12):** Instagram, Facebook (`letssmartshala`), LinkedIn
  (`company/smart-shala`) and WhatsApp, from `site.social`.
- **Domain (2026-10-12):** the site is live at `https://www.letssmartshala.com`
  (main address); `letssmartshala.com` redirects to it. DNS is at Hostinger;
  `app`, `api`, `backend` belong to the CRM. Canonical links, sitemap and
  share previews use the www address.
- **Leads (2026-10-12):** Resend domain verified, template
  `demo-lead-notification` published, CRM endpoint and token set in Vercel.
- **WhatsApp messages to parents (owner, 2026-10-12):** not needed — do not
  mention or claim it anywhere on the site.
- **Owner's preferences:**
  - Professional, not playful.
  - Floating elements are welcome, but they must not pull attention from the
    copy.
  - Nothing that loops in an annoying way.
  - **No scroll-jacking or pinned scroll-driven sections**, except the two
    the owner asked for: `04 Module tour` and `05 Student journey` pin on
    desktop, and the scroll steps through them.
  - Background patterns and school references: subtle and minimal.
  - Sections should fit the screen where possible. Longer is fine if it
    displays well, but nothing may be cut off.
  - Leave breathing room at the bottom of sections.
  - Two sections must not share the same layout.

## Home page sections (`src/pages/HomePage.jsx`)

Each section has a name (`data-section` on its root; the visible tags were
removed at the owner's request). Use these names with the owner: 01 Hero (day arc), 02 Four desks, 03 Phone feed, 04 Module tour,
05 Student journey, 06 Indian terms, 07 Your data, 08 FAQ chat,
09 Book a demo, 10 Footer.

1. `DayArcHero`: done; the owner likes it.
2. `Desks` (02 Four desks): a 2×2 grid; each card has a live preview on the
   left and text on the right. Each preview plays once, when its card is 60%
   on screen. Done.
3. `Stream`: modules flow on a curve into a floating principal's phone, and
   its feed fills up. Light theme. Liked.
4. `Tour` (04 Module tour): desktop **pins** and the scroll steps through
   the six modules (half a screen each). Phones autoplay once (4.2s each,
   with a timer line). The window is smaller (max 30rem), and a notification
   card per module (`note` in `src/data/tour.js`) pops out over its corner.
5. `Flow` (05 Student journey): **the owner likes this design; keep it.**
   Five stations on one line, with a wide student file under them (one
   column per station). A blue line travels station to station; each
   column fills in from placeholders as the line arrives, and PAID stamps
   at the end. Desktop **pins** and the scroll drives it; phones autoplay
   once when fully in view (`useAutoStep` `threshold` option).
6. `India`: a marquee of Indian school terms.
7. `Security`: three-schools diagram (your own database) plus three points.
8. `Faq`: chat-style; tapping a question shows a typing indicator, then the
   answer.
9. `CtaBand`: soft paper background with a blurred arc.
10. Footer: light.
