# Brag Plan: SmartShala

## What Is This App?
SmartShala is a school management CRM for Indian schools, covering admissions, attendance, fees, exams, transport and staff.

## The Angle
This is an original morning-in-motion product film, separate from the earlier paper-register story. A single school day moves through the people doing the work: the principal checks the overview, a teacher finishes attendance, an accountant records a payment, and the same student record reflects every action.

## Hook
`7:50 AM` and the line `Before the first bell, the whole school is already moving.`

## Key Moments
- Principal dashboard: staff in, attendance and fees today settle into view.
- Teacher marks Class 7-B attendance student by student.
- Accountant records a UPI payment and generates Receipt R-2041.
- Aarav Sharma's profile gathers the live results: 94% attendance, zero fee balance and Route 4.

## Outro
`Every office. One school.` followed by SmartShala and the demo URL.

## User Flow Worth Showing
Principal overview -> teacher marks attendance -> accountant records payment -> student profile updates across the school.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: a precise, optimistic school morning
- Interpretation: clean product windows, confident transitions, clear data and no abstract filler.

## Format
Landscape - 1920x1080

## Duration
21 seconds.

## Visual Identity
- Background: `#F5EFE6`
- Accent: `#003DE5`
- Text: `#0E1D61`
- Display/body: Plus Jakarta Sans-inspired system sans
- Strongest visual: the source site's clean navy CRM windows, blue controls and ivory field-notes palette

## Share Copy
At 7:50 AM, the whole school is already in motion. SmartShala keeps the principal, teacher and accountant working from the same live record.

## Audio Direction
- Role: clean, steady business bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, low beneath the data moments, fading at the end
- Music cue guidance: light feature reveals align to the track's natural pulse; principal overview, payment confirmation and final mark are the three strongest landings
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle background glow and dashboard emphasis only
- SFX posture: three restrained UI accents: dashboard land, payment confirmation and final mark

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Morning Starts - 3.4s
7:50 AM sits above a school-day arc. The hook appears with a soft dawn glow.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: calm beginning with one gentle introduction accent.
Transition: clean cut -> Scene 2.

### Scene 2 - Principal Overview - 3.6s
Principal dashboard reveals Staff in 38/41, Attendance 94%, and Fees today Rs 1,26,908. A live school-status rail fills.
Sequential/interaction: three metric cards land left to right and hold.
Audio intent: small card landing followed by an even music bed.
Transition: slide -> Scene 3.

### Scene 3 - Teacher Attendance - 3.2s
Class 7-B roll call marks Aarav, Ananya and Diya present, Arjun absent, then settles at 38 present.
Sequential/interaction: four attendance states appear one by one.
Audio intent: restrained click on the first and final state.
Transition: slide -> Scene 4.

### Scene 4 - Fees And Receipt - 3.8s
Collection Command Center records Aarav's Rs 12,500 UPI payment, stamps it paid and shows Receipt R-2041 PDF ready.
Sequential/interaction: a payment row confirms, then the receipt button resolves.
Audio intent: one success bell when the receipt becomes ready.
Transition: shared-record merge -> Scene 5.

### Scene 5 - One Student Record - 3.8s
Aarav Sharma's profile gathers the same data: Class 7-B, attendance 94%, balance Rs 0, Route 4, and APAAR ID linked.
Sequential/interaction: three profile status cards connect to the record.
Audio intent: music broadens while the connections settle.
Transition: hard but calm cut -> Scene 6.

### Scene 6 - End Card - 3.2s
`Every office. One school.` then SmartShala and `letssmartshala.com`.
Sequential/interaction: mark, name and URL resolve in order.
Audio intent: final bell then a short fade.

**Music mood:** steady, polished and optimistic.
