# Hyperframes Composition Brief: SmartShala Morning

## Objective
Create a new launch video unrelated to the earlier SmartShala paper-register animation. It should show one school morning through the actual product roles and sample data in the source project.

## Output
- Composition: `composition/`
- Render: `brag.mp4`, landscape, 1920x1080, 21 seconds
- Poster: settled SmartShala end card, baked into frame zero

## Source Material
- `src/data/home.js`: role moments and student journey
- `src/data/tour.js`: principal, attendance, fees, receipt and student-record content
- `src/styles/variables.css`: ivory, navy, logo blue, gold and status colors
- Product copy must stay factual and use only illustrative sample data already present in the project.

## Creative Direction
Use an original flat product-film system: rounded application windows, clear typography, a school-day timing cue, and exact data moments. Do not reuse the earlier story film's paper, hand-drawn registers, its camera motion or its end-card composition.

## Required Product Moments
1. Principal view: Staff in `38 / 41`, attendance `94%`, fees today `Rs 1,26,908`.
2. Teacher attendance: Class 7-B, Aarav Sharma, Ananya Iyer, Arjun Nair, Diya Mehta; final status `38 present`.
3. Fees: Aarav Sharma, UPI, `Rs 12,500`, `R-2041`, receipt PDF ready.
4. Student record: Class 7-B, Roll 12, APAAR ID linked, Route 4, attendance 94%, fee balance Rs 0.

## Audio
Use the supplied clean music bed and three light SFX. Let visual timing lead. Final music fades under the logo.

## Validation
Capture the canvas animation at 30fps, mix audio locally, inspect key scene frames, select a settled poster, and bake it into the first video frame.
