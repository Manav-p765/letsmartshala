# SmartShala Brag Plan

## Product
SmartShala brings admissions, attendance, fees, exams, transport and payroll into one school system for Indian schools.

## Angle
One student's details get copied across five registers, a roll number drifts, and the principal waits for the day's fees. SmartShala turns that paper trail into one shared student record for the whole school. The project already has a frame-accurate animation for this story, so the cut uses its real artwork, camera moves, transitions and original synthesized score rather than approximating its interface.

## Rubric
1. App: school management CRM for Indian schools.
2. Claim: one record is shared by every school role.
3. Visual hook: paper registers with matching entries that begin to disagree.
4. UI moment: the original film's SmartShala student record and role views.
5. Length: 25 seconds, the full authored story played at 1.9x.
6. Tone: polished, specific product story; paper-era motion settles into crisp CRM UI.
7. Audio: original synthesized score and action-matched sound effects, kept in sync with the animation.
8. Share caption: SmartShala turns admissions, attendance, fees, exams, transport and payroll into one shared school record.
9. User flow: admission is recorded, class and fee details build the student file, then offices read the same record and receipt.
