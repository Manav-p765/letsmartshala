# Composition Brief: SmartShala

## Objective
Create a 25-second shareable product film that accurately follows SmartShala's existing authored animation and shows how one student record is shared across school roles.

## Output
- Composition: `composition/`
- Video: `brag.mp4`, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 25 seconds
- Poster: `brag.jpg`, selected from a settled product reveal and baked into frame zero

## Source material
- Project root: `D:/work/smart-shala`
- Primary visual source: `public/film/smartshala-story.html`
- Product data and story: `film/storyboard.md`, `src/data/home.js`, `src/components/Home/Desks.jsx`, `src/components/Home/Flow.jsx`
- Product: SmartShala, school management CRM for Indian schools
- Visual claim: five registers become one shared student record

## Creative direction
- Tone: polished, human, specific to school office work
- Hook: one student's name gets copied across paper registers
- Payoff: the roll mismatch resolves into one live record seen by four roles
- Avoid invented screen layouts, generic SaaS panels, and unsupported features

## Visual identity
- Ivory paper: `#F5EFE6`; warm white: `#FFFDF6`; navy: `#0E1D61`; logo blue: `#003DE5`; gold: `#B8964F`; error red: `#C8242C`
- Typography and linework follow the source film
- Use the original hand-drawn paper animation, transition, crisp CRM record, role views, wordmark and CTA

## Motion and audio
Use the source film's deterministic frame drawing and matching synthesized score/SFX. Preserve the authored camera movement, register writing, red mismatch mark, record assembly, role reveals and final lockup. Set playback speed to 1.9x for a 25-second cut. Keep its original sample data and score cues synchronized.

## Delivery
Render locally, inspect representative frames, select a settled product frame for the poster, bake that still into frame zero, and include concise share copy. Do not publish or upload.
